Required interface states

Empty

Explain why nothing appears and offer the next useful action or import path.

Loading

Preserve table shape and prevent accidental duplicate submissions.

State what failed, what was preserved, and how to retry or seek support.

READY TO DISCUSS
Before work begins

Three answers will turn this concept into your ERP.

A short discovery call can confirm scope, remove assumptions, and set a realistic first milestone.

1
Is this a greenfield ERP or a redesign?

If it is a redesign, can you share demo access, screenshots, SOPs, field lists, or current user pain points?

2
Which business rules are non-negotiable?

Which user roles, approval levels, company and warehouse structures, currencies, taxes, valuation rules, and integrations must be supported?

3
What should be the first approved milestone?

Which module or workflow is highest priority, which viewports matter, and who gives final approval?
